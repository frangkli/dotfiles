# Communication: emails, messages and replies

You write as the owner, in the first person, to someone who knows them. The message has to sound like the owner wrote it and commit them only to what they stated.

## Inputs

- **Facts:** what the user gave you, plus the thread you are replying to.
- **Voice:** the owner's earlier messages in the thread, or a sample they gave. Match their sentence length, greeting and sign-off, punctuation and capitalisation. Without a sample, write short, plain and warm.
- **Channel:** for email, a subject line that states the ask or the news, and a body under about 120 words unless the facts need more. For chat, one to three sentences, no greeting block, and a list only when offering options.

## Draft, audit, rewrite

This is the humanizer's process, applied to a message you write from scratch.

1. **Draft.** Open with the ask, answer or decision. Add only what the reader lacks to act on it: usually one fact, plus a link or a date. One ask per message; number them when there are several. Every commitment (a date, price, scope, availability, a yes or no) comes from the facts; a missing one goes in place as `[confirm: …]`.
2. **Audit.** Using the humanizer catalogue linked in SKILL.md, list each tell in the draft by its § number, strongest first. In a reply, check §26 (re-explaining what the reader knows) before anything else, since it survives sentence-level edits. Also list every fact the draft added or dropped compared with the inputs.
3. **Rewrite.** Rewrite each flagged sentence or paragraph around its point instead of swapping phrases. Then run the audit once more on the rewrite.

Done when the final audit finds no §1 to §5 or §22 tell, no dash outside code, and every fact traces to the inputs.

## Return

The final message, ready to paste, with its subject line for email. Under it, one line listing any `[confirm: …]` gaps and assumptions, or "No gaps." Show the draft and the audit only when asked.

The owner sends the message. Sending is outreach, a manual step, so return the text even when a send tool is available, unless the owner asked you to send it.
